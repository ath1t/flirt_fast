# Flirt Fast

> *no notifications. no backspace. just a lamp and the page.*

**Flirt Fast** is a minimalist, distraction-free typewriter and typing tutor built as a single-file web application. Designed around the warm intimacy of a late-night desk lamp on paper, it strips away cluttered dashboards and progress bars in favor of a tactile platen line, sliding carriage pill, progressive upward line fading, and synthesized mechanical typewriter audio.

---

## Features

- **Lamp & Paper Atmosphere**: Muted slate-blue paper canvas illuminated by an ambient radial lamp glow centered over your active typing position, with three switchable lighting moods (**Slate**, **Amber**, and **Midnight**).
- **Platen Line & Sliding Carriage**: A full-width horizontal platen baseline with margin ticks and a pill-shaped carriage indicator that tracks your horizontal progress across every line.
- **Progressive History Fade**: Completed lines scroll upward smoothly, gradually fading in opacity and softening into subtle atmospheric blur—keeping your eyes anchored strictly to the current line.
- **Two Typing Disciplines**:
  - **Forward-Flow (No Backspace)**: True typewriter momentum—`Backspace` and cursor repositioning are locked with a tactile carriage shake and mechanical thud, recording errors inline without breaking rhythm.
  - **Strict Mode**: Requires accurate keystrokes before advancing the carriage.
- **Curated Practice Packs**: Switch between themed typing sessions, monitor live **WPM** and **Accuracy** telemetry, and export a `.txt` session summary report upon completion.
- **Synthesized Web Audio Engine**: Real-time procedural mechanical keystrokes, carriage return bells, and lock thuds powered by the Web Audio API—zero external audio files required.
- **Zero-Clutter Auto-Hiding HUD**: All controls stay invisible while typing and appear smoothly when moving your pointer near the top or bottom edges of the viewport.
